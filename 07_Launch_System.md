# Launch System

## Reference Product

AI SEO Audit Platform for Ecommerce Stores

## Launch Positioning

A focused SEO blueprint tool for ecommerce founders who need to know what to fix before wasting money on ads, apps, or developers.

## Primary Audience

- Shopify store owners
- ecommerce founders
- small agencies
- product page optimization consultants
- store owners with low organic traffic

## Core Offer

Get a prioritized SEO blueprint for your store.

Not a dashboard.
Not a generic report.
A clear fix order.

## Conversion Trigger

The user should feel:

“Now I know what to fix first.”

## First Channels

### LinkedIn

Audience:
founders, ecommerce consultants, solo operators

Post angle:
Most ecommerce stores do not need more traffic first. They need to know what is blocking visibility.

### Reddit / Communities

Audience:
Shopify, ecommerce, small business, SaaS builders

Post angle:
I built a simple way to turn a store URL into a prioritized SEO fix list.

### Direct Outreach

Audience:
small store owners with visible SEO weaknesses

Message angle:
I noticed your store may have fixable SEO structure issues. This tool gives a prioritized blueprint.
